# Part 2 — Core diatonic progression set

The Verse, Chorus, and Middle 8 use the same five-chord pool in C major: I, ii, IV, V, and vi. Play the notated triads first as written to hear each harmonic function. Then sustain or sing one chord tone while another player, keyboard, or backing track supplies the progression.

## 2.1 Verse — I–vi–IV–V

This familiar progression moves away from tonic through the relative minor before IV and V prepare the return to C.

## 2.2 Chorus — IV–I–V–vi

Notice how beginning on IV makes the phrase feel less settled. Compare the arrival on I in bar 2 with the softer continuation to vi in bar 4.

## 2.3 Middle 8 — vi–IV–ii–V

This section begins away from tonic and gathers momentum through ii–V. Loop all three sections, identifying the bass/root and your own chord tone before each change.
