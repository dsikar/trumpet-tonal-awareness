# Part 1 — Daily warm-up foundation

These are short baseline materials for a Bb trumpet (written pitch). Keep the sound centred and comfortable; stop before fatigue changes the response. Use a tuner and recorder occasionally, but prioritise steady air, relaxed embouchure, and an even release.

## 1.1 Long tones

Play each whole note at mezzo-piano, crescendo slightly through the middle, then return to mezzo-piano. Rest for one bar between repetitions if needed. Move the pattern by semitone only when tone and intonation remain stable.

## 1.2 Lip slurs / overtone slurs

Slur every note without tongue. Keep the air moving through the change and avoid forcing the upper note. Begin softly, then extend the pattern only within the day’s comfortable register.

## 1.3 Scales

Use one key per day. The written C-major example is a range and fingering baseline; transpose it through major and the three minor forms, then add chromatic scale work at the same tempo.

## 1.4 Tonguing patterns

Maintain one unbroken air stream. The first bar is single tongue, the second double tongue, and the third triple tongue. Begin where syllables stay clear; do not trade clarity for speed.

## 1.5 Arpeggios

This is root-position baseline work only. Slur first for evenness, then tongue lightly. Full inversion studies belong in Part 4.
